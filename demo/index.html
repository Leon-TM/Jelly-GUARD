Jelly Guard – Demo Prototype

Concept images of the early-warning system for jellyfish blooms, developed for the Blue-Cloud Hackathon 2025.

How it works

Jolly Guard collects marine and satellite data from Blue-Cloud services and other sources. The system processes these datasets with machine learning models to predict jellyfish blooms.

Forecast results are displayed on interactive maps and transformed into alerts for end users. The goal is to support early-warning, increase marine safety, and protect local economies.
