Protecting Marine Ecosystems and Human Safety from Jellyfish Blooms

Jolly Guard is an innovative early warning system that combines satellite data, oceanographic models, and machine learning to predict jellyfish blooms and alert coastal communities, fishermen, and tourists.

View Our Prototype Hackathon Submission

The Problem We're Solving

Jellyfish blooms are increasing globally due to climate change, overfishing, and coastal development. These blooms pose significant threats to:

  • Marine ecosystems: Jellyfish can outcompete fish for resources and prey on fish eggs and larvae
  • Coastal economies: Tourism suffers when beaches are closed due to jellyfish invasions
  • Fisheries: Jellyfish clog fishing nets and damage catches
  • Human health: Some jellyfish species deliver painful, sometimes dangerous stings
  • Infrastructure: Jellyfish can clog cooling systems of power plants and desalination facilities

Current monitoring methods are reactive rather than predictive, leaving communities vulnerable to sudden jellyfish invasions.

Our Innovative Solution

🌊

Data Integration

Combines Blue-Cloud marine data with satellite imagery and oceanographic models to track jellyfish-friendly conditions.

🤖

AI Prediction

Machine learning algorithms analyze historical and real-time data to forecast jellyfish bloom formation and movement.

📱

Early Warning System

Alerts are sent to coastal communities, fishermen, and tourists via mobile apps and web platforms.
